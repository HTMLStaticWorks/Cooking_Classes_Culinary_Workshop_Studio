The Physics of Egg Yolk Pasta Hydration

By Chef Matteao Rossi • Published Aug 14, 2026

Fresh Italian pasta isn’t merely flour and water—it’s an intricate chemical matrix of glutenin and gliadin proteins bonded with lipid fats.

When using 100% egg yolks rather than whole eggs, the water content drops from 75% down to 50%, while lecithin and fat percentages double. This creates a silkier sfoglia dough sheet that rolls thinner without tearing.
